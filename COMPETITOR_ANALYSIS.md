# Competitor Analysis: thefabricstore.pk

## What they do well

- Large product catalog depth
- Clear discount visibility
- Frequent category-based merchandising
- Familiar ecommerce structure users already understand

## Weaknesses observed

- Repetitive content patterns make browsing tiring
- Information density is high, visual hierarchy is weak
- Navigation and category naming can feel overloaded
- Product discovery lacks refined filtering UX
- Mobile experience can feel cluttered in long product streams

## Opportunities for Qissa Wear

- More editorial, premium visual identity (fashion-first)
- Better product discovery with cleaner filters + search
- Stronger category architecture and reduced repetition
- Faster perceived performance and smoother transitions
- Better admin workflow for managing catalog growth

## Frontend Direction (Unique, Modern, Better)

- **Design language**: editorial + minimal luxury (not template-like)
- **Typography**: premium serif + clean sans pairing
- **Color system**: warm neutrals, deep charcoal, accent rose-gold
- **Homepage**:
  - Story-led hero with clear CTA
  - Curated collections section
  - Best sellers with quick actions
  - Value strip (shipping, returns, support)
- **Category pages**:
  - Sticky filter drawer on mobile
  - Left filter panel on desktop
  - Sort + chips for active filters
  - Pagination or load more with skeleton states
- **Product detail page**:
  - Large gallery with fabric-focused zoom
  - Size/care/fabric detail tabs
  - Related products and recently viewed

## Feature Improvements Over Competitor

- Wishlist
- Product reviews and ratings
- Stock badges (low stock, sold out)
- Smarter search with typo tolerance (future)
- Admin analytics for inventory and order trends

## MVP Scope for Better-Than-Competitor Launch

- High-quality homepage and category UX
- Reliable auth and cart flow
- Clean admin CRUD for fast catalog updates
- Search + filtering that feels instant on mobile and desktop
